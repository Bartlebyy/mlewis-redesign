Latest production · 2026

May I Come In

In 2026, Mike Lewis produced the jazz album May I Come In, featuring singer Lisanne Lyons, released on OA2 Records and available on all major streaming platforms. The album showcases Lewis's jazz orchestra arrangements, performed by leading musicians from Miami and New York — ranging from the drive of “The Song Is You” to the lyricism of “This Is Always.”

Featured soloists: Ed Calle (saxophone), Francisco Dimas (trumpet), John Hart (guitar), Billy Ross (flute), John Toomey (piano).

Sparkling, scintillating jazz singing — prepared to hear it differently than usual. All Music Guide
