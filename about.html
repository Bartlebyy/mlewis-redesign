About

This is what I do.

In music since 1956, arranging, conducting and producing since 1970, with a performing career before that shared stages with Sinatra, Sammy Davis Jr. and Ray Charles.

Highlights · 1970–2026

Highlights from the freelance years

A selection from 56 years freelancing as an arranger, conductor and producer. The full chronological story, from birth to now, is further down this page.

Many of these recordings were big hits. Mike wrote all the charts, horns and strings, and played tenor and alto on the sessions… I don’t know when the guy ever slept. He invented the word ‘workaholic.’ Whit Sidener, Director of Studio Music & Jazz, University of Miami Frost School of Music
Mike is an expert at managing a recording session, maximizing the time and budget while extracting the most musical product for his client. Steve Sigmund, trombonist & contractor
1970

Fountainhead, Orchestration ’70

Produced the album at Criteria Studios, Miami, with David Glatt; released with help from producer Teo Macero (Miles Davis’s Bitches Brew).

1971

“Clean Up Woman,” Betty Wright

Arranged the horns on Wright’s first recording for Tone Distributors (RIAA Gold, No. 6 on the Hot 100), and the start of a decade-long run as Tone’s house arranger.

1972

“Too Late to Turn Back Now,” The Cornelius Brothers & Sister Rose

Played piano and arranged strings on the session. No. 2 on the Hot 100, RIAA Gold.

Mid-1970s

KC and the Sunshine Band

Horn and string arrangements for “Get Down Tonight,” “Boogie Shoes,” “I’m Your Boogie Man,” “Keep It Comin’ Love,” “That’s the Way (I Like It),” and “Please Don’t Go.” “Get Down Tonight” and “That’s the Way (I Like It)” are RIAA Diamond-certified, “Boogie Shoes” Platinum, and “I’m Your Boogie Man” Gold.

1975 – 1976

Bee Gees / Barry Gibb

String arrangements recorded at Criteria Studios, Miami, during the Bee Gees’ relocation to the Miami area.

1977

Crosby, Stills & Nash, CSN

String arrangements on “Cathedral,” “Cold Rain,” and “I Give You Give Blind.” The album is RIAA 4× Platinum.

Mid–late 1970s

Rod Stewart & Willie Nelson, Atlantic Records

String arrangements recorded at Criteria for producer Tom Dowd, including Rod Stewart’s “Tonight’s the Night.”

1978

Dr. Hook, Pleasure and Pain

Arrangements for the album featuring “When You’re in Love with a Beautiful Woman,” recorded in Nashville with overdubs in Miami. RIAA Gold, 1979. Hear it on the Listen page.

1978

“What You Won’t Do for Love,” Bobby Caldwell

Horn and string arrangements, including the brass melody that became one of the track’s most recognizable hooks. No. 9 on the Hot 100. Hear it on the Listen page.

1979

Anita Ward, Songs of Love

Strings and horns arrangements for the album, which includes “Ring My Bell,” No. 1 on the Hot 100, the UK Singles Chart, and Billboard’s R&B and Disco charts.

Feb 1990

First cruise line commission: Royal Caribbean

Began producing and arranging pre-recorded stage-show scores for Royal Caribbean Cruise Lines, the start of two decades of cruise-line production work.

2001

Sampled on Trick Daddy’s “Take It to da House”

The single builds its hook around Mike’s horn arrangement for KC and the Sunshine Band’s “Boogie Shoes” (1975), from the album Thugs Are Us, RIAA Platinum, No. 4 on the Billboard 200.

2002

Horn arrangements for P. Diddy, The Saga Continues…

RIAA Platinum, No. 2 on the Billboard 200.

2011

Begins working with Dr. Lisanne Lyons

Wrote a full show of jazz orchestra arrangements for Lyons’s performance with the Sunrise Pops Symphony Orchestra, the start of a long-running collaboration.

2017

Begins the MLewisPops project

Began arranging jazz and film-songbook standards for symphony orchestra, sequenced in Digital Performer, the source of the MIDI sequences on the Listen page.

Biography

A lifetime, in order

From a horn player sharing stages with Sinatra and Ray Charles to decades behind the pen as an arranger, conductor and producer: the full story, chronologically.

Aug 1938

Born in Danville, Virginia

1956 – 1960

Florida State University

Composition major.

June 1960

Joined the Charlie Spivak Orchestra

First professional job, playing second alto sax and writing arrangements for the band on tour.

Oct 1960 – Oct 1963

U.S. Army

First Army Band, Ft. Jay, New York, and 7th Army Band, Stuttgart, West Germany.

Nov 1963 – Jun 1964

Jimmy Dorsey Orchestra

Saxophonist and arranger, touring coast-to-coast.

Sep 1964 – Jun 1965

University of Miami

Music Education degree.

1965 – 1970

House orchestra, Deauville Hotel, Miami Beach

Backed touring headliners including Frank Sinatra, Tony Bennett, Sammy Davis Jr., the Supremes, the Temptations, Sonny & Cher, and Judy Garland.

1968 – 1970

Arranger, The Jackie Gleason TV Show

Miami Beach. Wrote arrangements on tight weekly deadlines for guests including Joel Grey, Liberace, Gene Kelly, and Milton Berle.

1970 – Present

Freelance arranger, conductor & producer

Most recently, produced May I Come In (Lisanne Lyons, OA2 Records, 2026). Hear it on the Listen page.
