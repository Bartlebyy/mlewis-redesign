This is what I do.

Arranger, conductor and producer since 1970 — with a performing career before that shared stages with Sinatra, Sammy Davis Jr. and Ray Charles.

Recent work · 2026

May I Come In

Lewis produced the jazz album May I Come In, featuring singer Lisanne Lyons, released on OA2 Records. The project presents Lyons in richly crafted jazz orchestra arrangements written and produced by Lewis, performed by premier musicians from Miami and New York — spanning the driving energy of “The Song Is You” to the lush lyricism of “This Is Always.”

Featured soloists: Ed Calle (saxophone), Francisco Dimas (trumpet), John Hart (guitar), Billy Ross (flute), John Toomey (piano).

Biography

Six decades, in order

Photo Mike Lewis — drop into /img/mike-lewis-bio.jpg

From a horn player sharing stages with Sinatra and Ray Charles to six decades behind the pen as an arranger, conductor and producer — a career told chronologically below.

Mike Lewis has scored for symphony pops orchestras, Broadway-style revues and cruise-line showrooms alike, building a reputation as an arranger who can move fluidly between genres without losing the room.
