# Mike Lewis book — source note

Mike's autobiography, *Faster Than Anybody Better, Better Than Anybody Faster*, was
supplied as a transcript excerpt (pages 14–15, 19–31, 37–95) and used as a source for
factual details on the site: the early-career timeline on `about.html` (FSU, Charlie
Spivak Orchestra, U.S. Army, Jimmy Dorsey Orchestra, Deauville Hotel, Jackie Gleason
Show), the Betty Wright / Tone Distributors correction, and the Cornelius Brothers &
Sister Rose and Trick Daddy sample entries in the Highlights section.

The full transcript is not stored in this repo (it's Mike's copyrighted text) — only
the factual, professional details drawn from it were used in site copy, per his own
request to keep the tone strictly professional (no personal anecdotes, grievances, or
color from the book was carried onto the site). If further detail passes are wanted
later, the transcript would need to be supplied again.
